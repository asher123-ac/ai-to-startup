## 1. The idea

CampusCrave is created for university students that can get really good deals at restaurants by using the food that had already been made and eventually were to be thrown out. This allows students to get food for a relatively fair price since every university student is looking for a good deal. This also allows restaurants to get a bit more value out of food that was going to be wasted by discounting it before they close for the day. This website is very useful for students, instead of looking around for deals on different apps, it shows participating restaurants that have a good deal for a cheap price. The website makes revenue by taking a small percentage of the purchase that's been made, this still allows restaurants to create value from the website because it is food that was already going to be wasted. Students are broke so this would be very beneficial for them to get something to eat if they didn't want to cook. With this website it solves a few problems, it allows for limited food waste and extra value for restaurants, and helps students save money.

## 2. Where IT creates value

CampusCrave acts as a platform that brings students and restaurants together, it organizes deals and makes information accessible in one place, this is a form of information system. The website makes posting and finding deals faster than looking for one manually, this shows process efficiency because it allows students to save time trying to look for the cheapest deal on other food websites and for restaurants to list deals. Data and information is shown by deals, prices, availability, and restaurant information being constantly organized and updated so that students can see what's available to them when they try to buy food. This creates less of a hassle to find something good to eat because restaurants can update when something sells out and it is all organized on one website so you don't have to search around for the next best deal. This allows for students to have better customer experience if things are updated on time so it doesn't show something that is already sold out, a good experience can lead to students coming back to the website.

## 3. Network effects

CampusCrave is a two-sided market. There's the student side and the restaurant side, students want cheap food and also a variety of choices that are convenient to them, restaurants want to make value off of food going to waste, more customers getting their food, and want an easy way to advertise deals. As the network grows, extra value can be created, the more restaurants that participate in the website, the more deals students will have to choose from. If more students start using it then there is also more potential buyers giving restaurants more reason to use the website to post their leftover food. The more activity on the platform, the more useful it becomes for both sides of the market. This cross-side network effect allows for profit on both ends. the problem at the start would be who would the website attracts first. By recruiting a few restaurants local to a campus that regularly have leftover food, it can be a starting base to attract students to use the website and see how it works. Eventually, with more people knowing about it, more restaurants may participate with deals on the website too. The restaurant deals posted on the website can attract students to buy them, and with different food options and prices on the website it gives students more variety on what they can purchase. Allowing students to see multiple deals in one place can attract more people and more restaurants to use this website.

## 4. Can it be copied? (the moat question)

This website creates value in a few ways, students are able to get cheap and affordable meals that won't break their bank account and are able to get something they actually want to eat. they can save time by only looking in one website rather than searching around other food websites trying to find the cheapest thing they can buy. Restaurants are able to reduce food waste by discounting it and maximize on profit, this kills two birds with one stone, they benefit financially as well as not wasting as much food.

I think CampusCrave isn't particularly rare right now, there are some apps that are similar to CampusCrave but different in some ways. CampusCrave specifically targets university students trying to get good deals on leftover food so it would target restaurants that are relatively close to a university or college campus. The website being made for Students specifically makes it a bit different compared to other websites.

The website itself is easy to copy, but to make it hard to imitate would be by building a relationship among students and restaurants to keep using this website and make it grow so that people find that switching to a different website would not be worth it. If students are constantly getting very good deals and restaurants are reducing waste and making profit, it creates a switching cost for the students and restaurants.

This website isn't relatively hard to replace, there are many other options for students to eat like cooking your own food or other places that may have a discount, but students may choose CampusCrave over other alternatives if they are unwilling to cook, or don't want to search for a deal to find somewhere else

A competitor with a bigger budget would need to recruit restaurants that are near a campus and would have to promote it to students to get them to use their platform instead and still be able to promote cheap deals, as well as enough restaurants to keep students using theirs. Building a relationship with both sides of the market would also be important so that it keeps growing.

advantages that grow stronger over time would include the network, brand, relationships, and loyalty. With more students attracting restaurants, more restaurants will attract students making it grow. If CampusCrave is known for finding cheap food more people will know about it. Restaurants that become more familiar with the website will post more deals, and students will keep buying from it if they know they can find a cheap meal.


## 5. Cloud and operations

Restaurants would need accounts to post deals as well as students for them to see the deals, the deals need to be able to update in real time when it sells out, there needs to be a safe payment process, and the website needs to be able to stay online for people to use. It would need SaaS for it to be able to do payment processing and notifications and would also need PaaS so the website can run online. The benefits of using cloud services is that CampusCrave wouldn't have to build their own servers or payment system, which makes it cheaper at the start and still allowing the platform to handle customers.

Risks that apply on relying on them is that the student accounts would need to be protected so that their information and payment information is secure, students may not be able to purchase food if the services go offline, and the services may become more expensive as the platform grows.



## 6. Risks and ethics

Restaurants are responsible that they are selling food that is still good to eat so restaurants would be responsible that the deals that are posted are still safe for people to eat, the platform would have to make sure that they respond to certain reports of unsafe food. students information can be hacked or leaked which is why payment needs to be secure and not much information is used. a restaurant could post a deal that is unavailable or sold out which is not accurate, restaurants would have to ensure that what they post is updated real time and remove what's sold out. Students may rely on the platform for a cheap meal every time whether or not if they know if it will be available, so the platform would have to make sure that nothing is guaranteed and it can sell out at any time.
